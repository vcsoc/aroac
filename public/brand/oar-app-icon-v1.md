Pale lime #c4ed9e emblem on uniform opaque dark charcoal green #101513 background extending to all four square edges. Flat solid colors, crisp clean vector-like edges, strong readable silhouette for small launcher icons. No gradient, no texture, no white speckles, no glow, no shadows, no border, no mockup, no surrounding presentation. One finished square icon only.
